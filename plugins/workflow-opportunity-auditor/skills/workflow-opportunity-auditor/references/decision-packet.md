# Decision packet

Use this structure for the final artifact.

## Decision

- Disposition: reuse | configure | integrate | extend | experiment | build | do not automate
- One-sentence rationale
- Confidence: high | medium | low
- Research date

## Outcome contract

- Affected person or customer
- Current result
- Target result and date
- Proof of success

## Current workflow and baseline

Summarize the trigger, accountable owner, key steps, handoffs, exceptions, approvals, completion receipt, measured values, estimates, and unknowns.

## Friction with evidence

List the highest-cost delay, error, repeated work, or missing handoff and cite the example or source.

## Options reviewed

Include the comparison table from the research gate with direct source links. Explain why each rejected option failed an outcome or constraint.

## Economics

Show inputs, formulas, results, and unknowns. Keep labor savings, avoided errors, risk reduction, and revenue effects separate. Never combine speculative benefits into one precise ROI number.

## Recommendation

State what to change first. For a build, name the exact missing layer and interfaces that remain owned by existing systems.

## 30-day experiment

- Scope and owner
- Five or fewer implementation steps
- Human checkpoints
- Baseline and target
- Evidence collection
- Rollback
- Review date

## Continue, change, or stop

- Continue when
- Change direction when
- Stop when

## Open risks and accountable humans

List privacy, security, financial, legal, customer, and operational risks with the person responsible for each decision.
