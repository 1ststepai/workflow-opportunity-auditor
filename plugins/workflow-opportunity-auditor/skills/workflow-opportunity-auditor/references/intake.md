# Workflow intake

Capture the minimum information needed to make the decision. Do not turn the audit into a long questionnaire when existing material answers these fields.

## Outcome

- Business outcome
- Person or customer affected
- Current result
- Target result
- Target date
- Evidence that proves the result

## Current workflow

- Trigger
- Accountable owner
- Participants and handoffs
- Inputs and source systems
- Ordered steps and decision points
- Exceptions and rework
- Approval points
- Output and destination
- Receipt or proof of completion

## Baseline

- Frequency per day, week, or month
- Minutes per run
- People involved per run
- Fully loaded hourly cost, when the user wants a labor-cost estimate
- Error, exception, or rework rate
- Average delay or cycle time
- Tool costs
- Revenue, risk, or customer effect when it can be measured responsibly

Record each field as `measured`, `estimated`, or `unknown`, with the source and date when available.

## Constraints

- Data sensitivity and retention requirements
- Required systems and prohibited providers
- Legal, contractual, or regulatory boundaries
- Human approvals that must remain
- Budget and implementation capacity
- Deadline
- Reversibility and rollback needs

## Friction log

Ask for one or two recent examples. Capture where work waited, failed, repeated, or required a person to reconcile conflicting information. Real examples outweigh general impressions.
